FreeFeed Privacy Policy

Effective August 12, 2026

FreeFeed is designed to work without sending your personal information or Instagram activity to the developer. Its settings and temporary-access timer stay in your browser.

At a glance

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics, advertising trackers, telemetry, or developer server are used.
  • No personal information or Instagram content is sent to the developer or another third party.
  • FreeFeed settings are stored locally by Chrome on your device.
  • FreeFeed does not sell, rent, or share user data.

Information FreeFeed handles

Settings and temporary access

FreeFeed stores your choices about which Instagram activities are available and, when you temporarily unlock normal Instagram, the time at which that access should end. This information is stored through Chrome's local extension storage on your device. The developer does not receive it.

Instagram page information processed locally

While you use instagram.com, FreeFeed reads the current page address and limited page-interface elements needed to identify, hide, show, or open the features you selected. This processing happens inside your browser. FreeFeed does not save or transmit the contents of your feed, messages, searches, notifications, stories, profile, uploaded media, or Instagram credentials.

Information not collected

FreeFeed does not collect your name, email address, IP address, precise location, financial information, authentication information, browsing history outside instagram.com, or usage analytics. It does not use cookies, fingerprinting, advertising identifiers, or remote analytics services.

How information is used and shared

The locally handled information is used only to remember your choices, enforce them on Instagram, display enabled activities, and end temporary access at the time you selected. It is not used for profiling, advertising, credit decisions, or another unrelated purpose. FreeFeed does not send user data to the developer or any third party, and does not sell, rent, license, or otherwise disclose it.

Retention and deletion

Your activity settings remain in Chrome's local extension storage until you change them, clear the extension's data, or uninstall FreeFeed. A temporary-access deadline is removed when you lock Instagram manually or after it expires. You can delete all FreeFeed data by uninstalling the extension or clearing its stored data through Chrome.

Instagram and third-party services

FreeFeed changes how parts of Instagram's website are presented in your browser; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta. Your interactions with Instagram continue to be handled by Instagram under Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

FreeFeed's use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. FreeFeed uses information only as necessary to provide its disclosed single purpose.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when FreeFeed's features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the FreeFeed developer at ronanrocking@gmail.com.
