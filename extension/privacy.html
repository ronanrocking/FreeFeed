FreeFeed

Privacy Policy

Effective August 12, 2026

FreeFeed is designed to work without sending your personal information or Instagram activity to the developer. Its settings and temporary-access timer stay in your browser.

At a glance

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics, advertising trackers, or telemetry are included.
  • No personal information or Instagram content is sent to the developer.
  • Your FreeFeed settings are stored locally by Chrome on your device.
  • FreeFeed does not sell, rent, or share user data.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the FreeFeed Chrome extension. It explains what information the extension handles, why it handles it, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Information FreeFeed handles

Settings and temporary access

FreeFeed stores your choices about which Instagram activities are available and, when you unlock normal Instagram temporarily, the time at which that access should end. This information is stored through Chrome’s local extension storage on your device. The developer does not receive it.

Instagram page information processed locally

While you use instagram.com, FreeFeed reads the current Instagram page address and limited page-interface elements needed to identify, hide, show, or open the features you selected. This processing happens inside your browser. FreeFeed does not save or transmit the contents of your feed, messages, searches, notifications, stories, profile, uploaded media, or Instagram credentials.

Information not collected by the developer

FreeFeed does not collect your name, email address, IP address, precise location, financial information, authentication information, browsing history outside instagram.com, or usage analytics. FreeFeed does not use cookies, fingerprinting, advertising identifiers, or remote analytics services.

Browser access and permissions

Storage
Used only to retain your activity settings and temporary-access deadline on your device.
Access to instagram.com
Used only to replace or restrict selected Instagram interface areas and provide the FreeFeed home experience.

FreeFeed does not request access to other websites.

How information is used

The locally handled information is used only to provide FreeFeed’s user-facing purpose: remembering your choices, enforcing them on Instagram, displaying enabled activities, and ending temporary access at the time you selected. It is not used for profiling, advertising, credit decisions, or any purpose unrelated to FreeFeed.

Sharing, sale, and disclosure

FreeFeed does not send user data to the developer or any third party, and the developer does not sell, rent, license, or otherwise disclose user data. Because the developer does not receive your locally stored settings or locally processed Instagram page information, there is no developer-held user database to share.

Retention and deletion

Your activity settings remain in Chrome’s local extension storage until you change them, clear the extension’s data, or uninstall FreeFeed. A temporary-access deadline is removed when you lock Instagram manually or after the deadline expires. You can delete all FreeFeed data by uninstalling the extension or clearing its stored data through Chrome.

Instagram and third-party services

FreeFeed changes how parts of Instagram’s website are presented in your browser; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta. Your interactions with Instagram continue to be sent to and handled by Instagram under Meta’s own terms and privacy practices. FreeFeed does not control Instagram’s data collection.

Security

FreeFeed reduces privacy risk by keeping its settings and page processing on your device and by including no developer server, analytics SDK, or outbound data transmission. No software can guarantee absolute security, and the security of Chrome, your device, and Instagram remains outside FreeFeed’s control.

Children’s privacy

FreeFeed is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the developer does not collect personal information through FreeFeed, the developer cannot identify a user’s age.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

FreeFeed’s use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. FreeFeed uses information only as necessary to provide its disclosed single purpose.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when FreeFeed’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised, and material changes to data practices will be disclosed in the extension or its store listing before they take effect where required.
